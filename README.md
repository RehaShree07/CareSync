# CareSync AI 🏥

### Smart Medical Document & Expense Management

CareSync AI is a user-friendly medical management web application designed to help individuals organize their medical bills, manage treatment expenses, store medical documents, and understand medical terminology in a simple way.

The application provides a centralized interface for accessing medical information and managing healthcare-related records.

## ✨ Features

- **📄 Medical Bill Upload:** Upload medical bills and documents in supported formats.
- **🔐 Medical Document Vault:** Store and access uploaded medical documents through the browser.
- **💰 Treatment Expense Tracker:** Track medical expenses using bill amounts saved in the application.
- **🩺 Medical Guide:** Search for medical terms and view simple explanations.
- **📋 Bill Information:** Record details such as patient name, bill date, category, amount, and notes.
- **🖥️ User-Friendly Interface:** Simple dashboard for navigating between medical management features.
- **💾 Browser-Based Storage:** Store supported records locally in the browser for later access on the same browser and device.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Medical bills and healthcare documents can become difficult to organize, especially when people need to track treatment costs or understand unfamiliar medical terminology. CareSync AI aims to make these tasks easier by bringing medical document management, expense tracking, and medical information into one accessible web application.

## 💡 Our Solution

CareSync AI provides a centralized dashboard where users can upload medical bills, maintain medical records, track expenses, and search for explanations of medical terms. The goal is to simplify medical record management and improve accessibility to basic medical information.

## 🔒 Privacy and Data Storage

CareSync AI is designed for browser-based record management. Uploaded files and saved records may be stored in the browser's local database rather than on a remote server.

- Data may not appear in another browser or device.
- Clearing browser data may remove locally stored records.
- This project does not automatically provide cloud backup or multi-device synchronization.
- Avoid uploading real patient information or sensitive medical documents to public repositories.

## ⚠️ Medical Disclaimer

CareSync AI is intended for medical document organization and general educational information. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making medical decisions. Medicine information and medical-term explanations should be verified with reliable healthcare sources.
